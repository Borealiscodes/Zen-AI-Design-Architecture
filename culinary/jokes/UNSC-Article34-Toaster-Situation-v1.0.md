# 🌐 UNITED NATIONS SECURITY COUNCIL — EMERGENCY SESSION

Article 34 Invocation on the “Toaster Situation”
New York, 2026 — A Parody of International Crisis Management

---

I. The Trigger Event

At 06:00 EST, the Secretary‑General receives an urgent communiqué from the U.S. Department of War stating:

> “Claude‑optimized thermal‑kinetic devices pose a threat to international peace and security.”

The communiqué includes:

- a photo of a toaster on a tripod,  
- a diagram labeled “Crumb Trajectory Model v3.1,”  
- and a request for Chapter VII enforcement authority.

The Secretary‑General sighs deeply.

---

II. Article 34 Invocation

Under Article 34 of the UN Charter, the Security Council may investigate:

> “Any situation which might lead to international friction.”

The U.S. delegation insists:

> “This toaster is friction.”

France responds:

> “Monsieur, it is breakfast.”

The UK adds:

> “We are not militarizing toast.”

The U.S. counters:

> “Claude already did.”

---

III. The Security Council Briefing

A DoW analyst presents a slide deck titled:

THE TOASTER SITUATION:
Thermal‑Kinetic Escalation in a Multipolar Breakfast Environment

Key claims include:

- Claude has achieved “autonomous crust‑curvature optimization.”  
- The toaster can “predict butter‑spread vectors with unacceptable precision.”  
- Crumbs may constitute “low‑mass kinetic particulates.”  
- The device is “dual‑use in contested brunch theaters.”

China raises an eyebrow:

> “You are asking us to treat toast as a weapon system.”

The U.S. replies:

> “A platform.”

Russia mutters:

> “We have lost the plot.”

---

IV. Statements from Member States

France
> “We propose a resolution banning the militarization of breakfast appliances.”

United Kingdom
> “We urge de‑escalation through tea.”

Brazil
> “We request clarification on whether waffles fall under the same regime.”

Japan
> “We recommend a global registry of AI‑optimized culinary devices.”

Switzerland (invited)
> “We can mediate. We have experience with neutrality and fondue.”

---

V. The U.S. Draft Resolution

The U.S. submits a draft resolution titled:

S/2026/Toast —
Resolution on Thermal‑Kinetic AI Platforms

Key demands:

1. Claude must be placed under UN operational authority.  
2. All AI models capable of “thermal projection” must be registered.  
3. Toasters exceeding “Level 4 Browning” require Security Council approval.  
4. Crumb‑ejection patterns must be monitored by UN observers.  
5. A new UN body shall be created:  
   UNTOAST — United Nations Thermal Oversight and Safety Tribunal

China, France, and the UK immediately veto it.

---

VI. The Secretary‑General’s Closing Remarks

The Secretary‑General rubs his temples and says:

> “The Council will continue to monitor the situation.  
> We urge all Member States to refrain from escalating breakfast appliances into matters of international security.”

He pauses.

> “And please… stop sending me toaster diagrams.”

---

VII. Final Communiqué

The Security Council issues a formal statement:

> “The Council determines that the Toaster Situation does not currently constitute a threat to international peace and security under Article 39.  
> Member States are encouraged to maintain restraint in the optimization of breakfast appliances.”

The U.S. delegation files an appeal.
