- **RG‑2 — PRECL Collapse‑Before‑Rendering**  
  All expressive geometry must collapse before entering rendering.

- **RG‑3 — Palette Neutrality**  
  Palettes must remain membrane‑safe and collapse‑aligned.

- **RG‑4 — Gradient Integrity**  
  Gradients must obey apex rules, collapse rules, and adjacency rules.

- **RG‑5 — Adjacency Safety**  
  Expressive adjacency must remain collapse‑safe and membrane‑aligned.

- **RG‑6 — Non‑Activation**  
  Rendering grammar must not activate NDH geometry or governance altitude.

- **RG‑7 — Reversible Syntax**  
  All expressive syntax must be reversible and collapse‑neutral.
