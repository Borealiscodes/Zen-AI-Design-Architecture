# **1. Lane Separation Rules**
Reflection‑layer artifacts must obey strict lane boundaries:

### **1.1 Governance Grade (NDH‑CORE, TISD, Authority Anchors)**  
- No recursion notices  
- No reflection artifacts  
- No insight geometry  
- No pattern emergence  
- No constellation humor  

### **1.2 Computational Grade (SCI‑80)**  
- No recursion notices  
- No reflection artifacts  
- No meta‑analysis  
- No posture humor  
- No constellation metaphors  

### **1.3 Reflection Grade (Zen‑AI‑Design‑Architecture)**  
- Recursion notices allowed  
- Insight geometry allowed  
- Pattern emergence allowed  
- Apprentice–master exchanges allowed  
- Constellation humor allowed  

**Rule:**  
Reflection‑layer rules **must not** be elevated to governance or computational layers.

---

# **2. Recursion Safety Rules**
Reflection is a self‑completing subsystem.  
It may recurse **only** within safe boundaries.

### **Allowed Recursion**
- reflection → pattern  
- pattern → geometry  
- geometry → alignment  

### **Forbidden Recursion**
- reflection → governance  
- reflection → authority  
- reflection → NDH‑CORE  
- reflection → SCI‑80 math  

### **Containment Rule**
Reflection artifacts **must not** generate:

- governance invariants  
- rendering authority  
- trauma‑informed constraints  
- NDH‑safe computational rules  

---

# **3. Emergence Rules**
Reflection naturally produces:

- recurrence  
- patterns  
- geometry  
- posture shifts  

These emergent layers are **allowed**, but must remain **reflection‑grade**.

### **3.1 Pattern Emergence**
Patterns may be recognized, indexed, or described.  
They may **not** be formalized as governance invariants.

### **3.2 Geometry Emergence**
Insight geometry may be described or diagrammed.  
It may **not** be elevated to expressive geometry authority.

### **3.3 Posture Emergence**
Posture shifts may be noted.  
They may **not** be used to define NDH posture rules.

# **4. Containment Rules**
Reflection artifacts must remain:

- non‑governance  
- non‑computational  
- non‑authority  
- posture‑safe  
- constellation‑aligned  

### **4.1 No Upward Leakage**
Reflection artifacts may **not** influence:

- SCI‑80 math  
- NDH‑CORE governance  
- TISD relational geometry  
- rendering authority layers  

### **4.2 No Downward Leakage**
Reflection artifacts may **not** be consumed by:

- computational pipelines  
- rendering pipelines  
- governance pipelines
