Expressive geometry expands first.  
Math compresses later.

This addendum clarifies **when** mathematical formalization should occur in the Sun–Moon–Earth triadic system and **why** it must follow the completion of all expressive layers.

Math is a *shadow* of meaning, not a generator of it.  
It arrives only after the triad has fully breathed.

## **IV. The Meta‑Insight — Why Math Is Last**

> **Math is the compression of meaning.

If math enters too early, it collapses the expressive field into literal geometry.  
If math enters after Dynamics, Cartography, and Ethos, it becomes a **beautiful formal shadow** of the mythic system.
