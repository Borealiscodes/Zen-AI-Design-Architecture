## **4. Rationale for the Separation**

Zenodo is a **research repository**, not a narrative or expressive archive.  
To maintain academic standards:

- mathematical content must be isolated  
- governance content must be formal  
- expressive content must be excluded  
- provenance must be stable  
- directory structure must be canonical  

NDH v1.1 is therefore restricted to the **Triadic + NDH mathematical engine** and the **NDH‑CORE governance spine**.

Expressive‑layer governance is important, but it is **not part of the mathematical publication envelope**.
