# 🌌 **Radial Cosmology Subsystem Glossary — v1.0**

## 🧭 **Cosmology Structure Terms**

- **Radial Cosmology** —  
  A geometric and narrative model organized in concentric rings around a central origin point.

- **Central Node** —  
  The cosmology’s origin point; anchor for all radial geometry and graph logic.

- **Mountain Ring** —  
  First ring around the central node; contains planetary and mythic mountains.

- **Story Ring** —  
  Second ring; contains narrative nodes derived from mountain symbolism.

- **Meta Ring** —  
  Outer ring; contains abstract thematic nodes (Breath, Fire, Silence, etc.).

- **Node Class** —  
  Category defining a node’s role: central, mountain, story, or meta.

- **Constellation** —  
  The full set of nodes and edges forming the cosmology’s graph.

---

## 🧩 **Graph & Logic Terms**

- **Adjacency** —  
  A permitted connection between two nodes in the cosmology graph.

- **Adjacency Matrix** —  
  Tabular representation of all node‑to‑node connections.

- **Edge** —  
  A line connecting two nodes; expresses logical or narrative linkage.

- **Graph Topology** —  
  The structural pattern formed by nodes and edges.

---

## 📐 **Geometry & Rendering Terms**

- **Radial Layout** —  
  A geometric system where nodes are placed at fixed radii from a center.

- **Coordinate Table** —  
  Cartesian coordinates for all nodes derived from radial geometry.

- **Rendering Spec** —  
  Defines radii, node sizes, colors, and stylistic rules.

- **JSON Rendering Manifest** —  
  Renderer‑agnostic configuration containing nodes, edges, coordinates, and styles.

- **SVG Geometry Spec** —  
  Vector blueprint encoding all nodes and edges with exact coordinates.

- **PNG Render** —  
  Final transparent Zen radial star chart.

---
