# 🌌 **Radial Cosmology Subsystem Design Law Codex — v1.0**

## 🧭 **Preamble**  
The Radial Cosmology subsystem operates under a set of architectural laws that ensure structural integrity, geometric consistency, rendering fidelity, and documentation discipline.  
These laws SHALL govern all present and future versions of the subsystem.

---

# 🏛️ **SECTION I — GEOMETRIC LAWS**

### **Law 1 — Radial Geometry Sovereignty**  
All cosmology structures SHALL be organized in concentric rings around a single **Central Node**.

### **Law 2 — Deterministic Coordinates**  
All node positions SHALL be derived exclusively from radial geometry and SHALL be recorded in the **Coordinate Table**.

### **Law 3 — Immutable Ring Hierarchy**  
The ring order — Central → Mountain → Story → Meta — SHALL remain stable across all versions.  
See: **Mountain Ring**

### **Law 4 — Node Class Integrity**  
Each node SHALL belong to exactly one **Node Class**, and class boundaries SHALL NOT overlap.

---

# 🧩 **SECTION II — GRAPH LOGIC LAWS**

### **Law 5 — Adjacency Primacy**  
All node connections SHALL be defined in the **Adjacency Matrix** and SHALL be preserved across all downstream artifacts.

### **Law 6 — Edge Determinism**  
Edges SHALL be generated solely from adjacency rules and SHALL NOT be manually altered in the SVG or PNG.

### **Law 7 — Graph Consistency**  
The constellation graph SHALL remain structurally identical across Spec → Manifest → SVG → PNG.  
See: **Constellation**

---

# 🎨 **SECTION III — RENDERING LAWS**

### **Law 8 — Manifest Supremacy**  
The **JSON Rendering Manifest** SHALL serve as the single source of truth for all rendering engines.

### **Law 9 — Vector Fidelity**  
The **SVG Geometry Spec** SHALL encode all geometry with exact coordinates and SHALL precede any raster output.

### **Law 10 — Transparency Requirement**  
All PNG renders SHALL use a transparent background unless explicitly overridden by a versioned style module.  
See: **PNG Render**

### **Law 11 — Style Cohesion**  
All renders SHALL adhere to the style rules defined in the Rendering Spec unless overridden by a versioned theme.

---

# 📘 **SECTION IV — DOCUMENTATION LAWS**

### **Law 12 — Lineage Completeness**  
Every artifact SHALL be documented in the **Master Index**.

### **Law 13 — Dependency Clarity**  
The dependency chain SHALL be preserved and documented in the **Render Summary**.

### **Law 14 — Subsystem Autonomy**  
The subsystem SHALL maintain its own README, FAQ, Glossary, Roadmap, Changelog, and Charter.  
See: **Subsystem README**

### **Law 15 — Provenance Enforcement**  
Every artifact SHALL include a provenance footer recording version, author, location, and timestamp.

---

# 🧪 **SECTION V — VERSIONING & GOVERNANCE LAWS**

### **Law 16 — Semantic Versioning**  
The subsystem SHALL use semantic versioning:  
- **1.x** — refinement and expansion  
- **2.x** — engine‑level capabilities  
See: **Subsystem Roadmap**

### **Law 17 — Backward Compatibility**  
Blueprint artifacts SHALL remain backward compatible across minor versions.

### **Law 18 — Forward Extensibility**  
New rings, node classes, or cosmologies SHALL be introduced through versioned specifications.

### **Law 19 — Subsystem Isolation**  
The subsystem SHALL remain isolated from other posture‑layer subsystems unless linked through a versioned interface.

### **Law 20 — Architectural Consistency**  
All subsystem artifacts SHALL follow posture‑layer documentation standards and SHALL NOT deviate from established structural patterns.
