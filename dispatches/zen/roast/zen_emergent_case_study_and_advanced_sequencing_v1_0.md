### 3. Color Fields → Hex Palette

Hex palettes cannot exist until color roles are defined.

### 4. Hex Palette → Provenance  
Provenance marks require:

- stable color  
- stable geometry  
- stable interaction  

Provenance is the **final seal**, not a design element.
