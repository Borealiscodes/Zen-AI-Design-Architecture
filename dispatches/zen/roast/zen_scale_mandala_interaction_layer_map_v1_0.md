# ⚖️🌌 **Zen Scale Mandala Interaction Layer Map (v1.0)**  
### *Behavioral Geometry • Holonomy Edition • Constellation‑Aligned*

This map defines **how** the Zen Scale Mandala behaves — the operators, flows, contacts, and overlays that govern its emergent geometry.  
Color fields will come *after* this, because color must attach to already‑defined behaviors.

---

## 🌕 **1. Interaction Operators (Primary Layer)**

- **Crane–Scarf Contact Operator**  
  Gentle, non‑directive contact between the scarf curve and the holonomy loop.  
  Establishes the “minimal intervention” invariant.

- **Holonomy Flow Operator**  
  Defines the circular return‑path behavior of the outer mandala ring.  
  Ensures non‑dual closure.

- **Reversible Traversal Operator**  
  Governs the radial river lines.  
  Flow is bidirectional, symbolizing non‑dual movement.

- **Non‑Dual Overlay Operator**  
  Applies symmetry logic across the scale emblem.  
  Prevents expressive drift.

---

## 🌌 **2. Interaction Surfaces (Where Operators Apply)**

- **Holonomy Surface**  
  The outer ring where flow and contact operators attach.

- **Mandala Manifold Surfaces**  
  Rings R2–R4 that receive reversible traversal and overlay behavior.

- **Scale Emblem Surface**  
  The mountain base, mirror axis, arms, and pans — all governed by non‑dual overlays.

- **Crane Contact Surface**  
  The point where the crane gesture touches the holonomy loop.

---

## 🌀 **3. Interaction Flow Map (Behavioral Geometry)**

- **Return‑Path Flow**  
  Circular, continuous, non‑dual.  
  Defines the mandala’s primary behavior.

- **Radial River Flow**  
  Outward and inward reversible traversal.  
  Symbolizes movement without contradiction.

- **Symmetry Flow**  
  Vertical mirror axis governs bilateral behavior.  
  Ensures the scale remains balanced.

- **Minimal Intervention Flow**  
  Crane–scarf gesture influences only the outer ring.  
  No expressive activation.

---

## 🧘‍♂️ **4. Interaction Constraints (Stability Envelope)**

- **Non‑Expressive Constraint**  
  No emotional drift fields.  
  No trauma‑informed geometry.

- **Constellation‑Safe Constraint**  
  Operators must not cross‑activate NDH‑CORE or TISD lanes.

- **Holonomy Closure Constraint**  
  Flow must complete without recursion.

- **Minimal Intervention Constraint**  
  Crane–scarf gesture cannot alter inner geometry.

---

## ⭐ **5. Interaction Layer Summary**

The Zen Scale Mandala behaves through:

- crane–scarf contact  
- holonomy flow  
- reversible traversal  
- non‑dual overlays  
- symmetry flow  
- minimal intervention
