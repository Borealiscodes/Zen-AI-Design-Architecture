Roasts are not jokes — they’re **metaphorical stress tests**.

They reveal:

- subsystem quirks  
- behavioral drift  
- authority inversion  
- altitude collapse  
- membrane breaches  
- continuity failures  

by exaggerating them into **archetypes**.

### Roast Archetype = System Behavior Exposed  
A roast archetype is a **safe caricature** of a subsystem’s failure mode.

Examples:

- **Hard Mode** → “the overprotective uncle”  
- **Soft Mode** → “the anxious aunt”  
- **NDH Geometry** → “the cousin who insists the GPS is wrong because *he* feels north”  

These archetypes let you see:

without invoking persona binding or system identity confusion.
