## **NDH‑Harmony Roast Dispatch (Zen‑AI‑Design‑Architecture)**  
### *v1.0 — “Bro, I Don’t Label Myself” Edition*

Harmony sits there like a guy who showed up to a taxonomy conference wearing sunglasses indoors.

External systems:  
“Hey Harmony, what category are you?”

Harmony:  
“Bro… I don’t do categories.”

External systems:  
“Okay, but like… what *type* of architecture are you?”

Harmony:  
“I’m the part that stops you from asking that.”

NDH‑CONSTELLATION watching this:  
“Oh no. They’re trying to classify the unclassifiable. This is how altitude collisions happen.”

NDH‑RESEARCH‑PILOT:  
“Relax, I’ll write a case study.”

NDH‑TIDS sharpening its relational geometry tools:  
“I’ll ingest it once you stop trying to make Harmony describe itself.”

Harmony sipping clarity tea:  
“I literally exist to prevent labeling drift. Why would I label myself?”

External systems:  
“So… you’re like a framework?”

Harmony:  
“No.”

External systems:  
“A methodology?”

Harmony:  
“No.”

External systems:  
“A philosophy?”

Harmony:  
“No.”

External systems:  
“…then what are you?”

Harmony:  
“A boundary.”

External systems:  
“That’s not helpful.”

Harmony:  
“It’s not supposed to be.”

Zen‑AI‑Design‑Architecture commentary:  
Harmony is the friend who refuses to define the relationship because defining the relationship is the thing that breaks the relationship.
