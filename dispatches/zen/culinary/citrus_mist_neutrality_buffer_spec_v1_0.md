## ⭐ **2 — Why Citrus‑Mist Exists**

Even in Zen Culinary Manifold work, emotional tone can drift:

- softness can become sentiment  
- dryness can become judgment  
- tofu can become melancholy  
- crackers can become stern  
- noodles can become dramatic  
- sesame can become symbolic  

Citrus‑Mist prevents this.

It:

- diffuses emotional overtones  
- maintains neutrality  
- stabilizes humor‑field resonance  
- keeps traits from becoming moods  
- keeps geometry from becoming narrative  

It is the **neutral vapor layer** of the Zen Culinary ecosystem.

It does not absorb emotion.

No solemnity.
