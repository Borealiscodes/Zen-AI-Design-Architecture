## 🧊 2 — Drift Factors (Primary)

Each snowflake’s drift is shaped by four canonical factors:

- **Temperature Gradient Vector**  
  Determines drift speed and wobble amplitude.

- **Humidity Differential**  
  Influences branching stability during drift.

- **Micro‑Turbulence Pockets**  
  Cause lateral jitter and rotational wobble.

- **Curvature Response**  
  Snowflakes with higher curvature respond more dramatically to turbulence.
