## ⭐ **3 — The Great Constitutional Geometry Debate**

The Founders gather around a giant **Constitutional Polygon**.

Hamilton insists the Constitution must be:

- a **triangle** (for stability)  
- pointing **upward** (for ambition)  
- with a **membrane‑safe perimeter** (for sovereignty)

Madison counters:

- “No, Alexander.  
  A Constitution must be a **pentagon**.  
  Five sides.  
  Five freedoms.  
  Five invariants.”

Hamilton responds:

> “James, that is absurd.  
> A pentagon is just a triangle with commitment issues.”

Jefferson, who has been silently sketching circles, finally speaks:

> “Gentlemen…  
> the Constitution is obviously a **circle**.  
> Perfect symmetry.  
> Infinite recursion.  
> No boundaries.”

Everyone stares at him.

Hamilton whispers:

> “Thomas…  
> circles are forbidden.  
> They cause **recursion collapse**.”

Jefferson shrugs.
