## ⭐ 1 — Log Identity Block  
Every incident log begins with a canonical identity header:

```
Incident ID: SNOW-DRIFT-#####
Timestamp: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS (Surface Manifold Time)
Snowflake Type: Canonical | Asymmetrical | Intersectional | Curvature-Heavy
Drift Mode: Helical | Mirror | Arc | Scatter
Severity Level: Frosty | Chilly | Icy | Glacial | Absolute Zero
```

This ensures the log is traceable, sortable, and NDH‑safe.

## 🧊 2 — Incident Trigger Section  
Record which emergency trigger initiated the log:

- **Jitter Spike** — wobble amplitude exceeded safe bounds  
- **Gradient Snap** — sudden temperature shear  
- **Branch Instability** — humidity shock  
- **Curvature Looping** — resonance amplification  
- **Scatter Cascade** — turbulence‑induced chaos  

Each trigger corresponds to a hazard zone in the  
**Snowflake Drift Hazard Map**.

## 🌬️ 3 — Environmental Conditions Snapshot  
Capture the intersecting environmental vectors at the moment of incident:

```
Temperature: -X°C
Humidity: Y%
Turbulence Intensity: Low | Medium | High | Chaotic
Curvature Amplification: Stable | Elevated | Critical
Micro-Environment Notes: (free text)
```

This snapshot explains *why* the drift destabilized.

## 🌀 4 — Drift Behavior Analysis  
A structured description of what the snowflake actually did:

```
Observed Drift Pattern:
- Lateral Jitter: <value>
- Rotational Wobble: <value>
- Drift Velocity: <value>
- Branch Response: Stable | Flexing | Collapsing
- Curvature Response: Neutral | Amplifying | Looping
```

This section mirrors the **Snowflake Drift Dynamics Report**.

---

## 🌌 5 — ERS (Emergency Response Sequence) Actions  
Document which ERS steps were executed:

- **ERS‑1 Orientation Stabilization**  
- **ERS‑2 Velocity Reduction**  
- **ERS‑3 Branch Integrity Check**  
- **ERS‑4 Curvature Dampening**  
- **ERS‑5 Environmental Reassessment**  
- **ERS‑6 Safe Zone Relocation**

Each action is logged as:

```
ERS Step: <step>
Status: Completed | Skipped | Failed
Notes: (free text)
```

Summarize the resolution:

```
Outcome:

- Relocated to Safe Zone
- Drift Neutralized
- Minor Structural Damage
- Major Structural Damage
- Snowflake Lost to Scatter Cascade
```
