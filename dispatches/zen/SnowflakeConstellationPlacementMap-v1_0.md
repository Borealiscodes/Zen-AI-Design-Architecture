## ⭐ 1 — Constellation Identity  
A snowflake constellation is defined as:

> **A distributed geometric cluster of unique snowflakes  
> placed across a cosmic manifold according to symmetry,  
> asymmetry, and intersectional growth history.**

This map places both:

- the **canonical sixfold snowflakes**  
- the **asymmetrical intersectional cousins**  

into a single cosmic‑cold starfield.

## 🧊 2 — Placement Rules (NDH‑Safe)

Each snowflake is placed according to:

- **Symmetry Vector** — canonical sixfold alignment  
- **Asymmetry Drift** — natural deviation from symmetry  
- **Intersectional Growth Factors** — temperature, humidity, micro‑environment  
- **Curvature Stability** — no singularities  
- **Freeze Compatibility** — temperature < 0°C  
- **ΔAltitude = 0** — surface manifold only  

These rules ensure the constellation is playful, not geometric‑activating.
