> “Hamilton, your triangle’s tiny — barely counts as Constitution,  
> Three sides? That’s a pamphlet — not a governmental solution.  
> And Jefferson, your circles? They’re just chaos in disguise —  
> No edges, no structure — that’s how democracies vaporize.”

> “I’m the pentagon tactician — five freedoms, five lines,

## 🎵 **3 — Jefferson: “Circle of Eternal Shade”**

Jefferson floats forward barefoot, quill glowing.

> “Hamilton, your angles hurt — too sharp, too uptight,  
> Your triangle’s a prison — no room for civil rights.  
> And Madison, your pentagon? Man, that thing’s a bureaucrat —  
> Five sides of paperwork — nobody wants to deal with that.”

> “My geometry’s infinite — recursion smooth as silk,  
> Your polygons are dairy farms — I’m the future, you’re just milk.”
