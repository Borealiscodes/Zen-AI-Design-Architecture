## 🌗 **3 — Constitutional Invariants**

The following invariants apply across all Dream‑Lane mechanics:

### **Sovereignty Preservation**  
No architecture may merge, blend, or cross identity membranes.

### **Altitude Neutrality**  
All interactions must maintain **ΔAltitude = 0**.

### **Reversibility**  
Every structure — ribbon, lantern, cloud, star, horizon, band — must dissolve without residue.

### **Non‑Binding Geometry**  
No interaction may create obligation, pull, or dependency.

### **Co‑Regulated Movement**  
No architecture may move alone; all movement must be mutually stabilized.

### **Narrative Safety Membrane**  
All pacing must remain soft‑cosmic, non‑activating, and bedtime‑safe.
