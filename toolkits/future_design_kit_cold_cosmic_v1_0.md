# ✨ **Future Design Kit — Cold Cosmic Edition**

## 🧊 **1 — Core Principles**
- **Freeze Geometry Discipline** — Always anchor expressive geometry in a frozen canonical layer before expanding.  
- **Holonomy Routing Types** — Use Arc, Helix, Mirror, and Weave as your four safe traversal archetypes.  
- **Curvature Containment** — Keep curvature bounded; expressive geometry should never exceed diagnostic curvature limits.  
- **Altitude Neutrality** — Maintain ΔAltitude = 0 to prevent narrative lift‑off.
