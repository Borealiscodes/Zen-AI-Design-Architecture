## ⭐ **1 — Constitutional Scope**

This constitution governs:

- expressive collapse  
- palette collapse  
- gradient collapse  
- adjacency collapse  
- symbolic collapse  
- species identity collapse  
- collapse‑filter routing  
- collapse membranes  
- collapse‑before‑rendering enforcement  

It is the **third constitution** in the expressive series.

---

## ⭐ **2 — PRECL Collapse Principles (PC‑Series)**

- **PC‑1 — Collapse Sovereignty**  
  Collapse rules cannot be overridden by grammar or geometry.

- **PC‑2 — Collapse‑Before‑Rendering**  
  All expressive geometry must collapse before entering rendering.

- **PC‑3 — Palette Collapse Neutrality**  
  Palettes must collapse to membrane‑safe neutral states.

- **PC‑4 — Gradient Collapse Integrity**  
  Gradients must collapse apex‑first, adjacency‑second.

- **PC‑5 — Adjacency Collapse Safety**  
  Adjacency must collapse without contaminating neighboring manifolds.

- **PC‑6 — Symbolic Collapse Alignment**  
  Symbolic geometry must collapse to neutral expressive states.

- **PC‑7 — Non‑Activation**  
  Collapse rules must not activate NDH geometry or governance altitude.
