This constitution governs:

- apex-stable routing  
- reversible routing pathways  
- expressive routing membranes

It is the **fifth and final constitution** in the expressive series.

---

## ⭐ **2 — Routing Principles (XR‑Series)**

- **XR‑1 — Routing Sovereignty**  
  Routing rules cannot be overridden by grammar, geometry, collapse, or visualization constitutions.

- **XR‑2 — Altitude Discipline**  
  Routing must remain strictly within A6–A8.

- **XR‑3 — Collapse‑Filter Routing**  
  Routing must pass through collapse filters before entering visualization or rendering.

- **XR‑4 — Membrane‑Aligned Routing**  
  Routing must obey expressive membranes and avoid structural or governance drift.

- **XR‑5 — Adjacency‑Safe Routing**  
  Routing must not contaminate neighboring expressive manifolds.

- **XR‑6 — Apex‑Stable Routing**  
  Routing must preserve apex gradient stability across expressive surfaces.

- **XR‑7 — Reversible Routing**  
  All expressive routing pathways must be reversible and collapse-neutral.

- **XR‑8 — Non‑Activation**  
  Routing must not activate NDH geometry or governance altitude.
