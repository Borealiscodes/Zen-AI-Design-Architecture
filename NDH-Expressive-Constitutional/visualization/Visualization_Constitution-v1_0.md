## ⭐ **1 — Constitutional Scope**

This constitution governs:

- visualization templates  
- palette-safe visualization  
- gradient-safe visualization  
- adjacency-safe visualization  
- apex visualization stability  
- collapse-before-visualization behavior  
- visualization membranes  
- expressive visualization routing  

It is the **fourth constitution** in the expressive series.

---

## ⭐ **2 — Visualization Principles (VZ‑Series)**

- **VZ‑1 — Visualization Sovereignty**  
  Visualization rules cannot be overridden by grammar, geometry, or collapse constitutions.

- **VZ‑2 — Collapse‑Before‑Visualization**  
  All expressive geometry must collapse before entering visualization.

- **VZ‑3 — Palette‑Safe Visualization**  
  Visualization must obey palette boundaries defined by PRECL.

- **VZ‑4 — Gradient‑Safe Visualization**  
  Gradients must obey apex rules, collapse rules, and adjacency rules.

- **VZ‑5 — Adjacency‑Safe Visualization**  
  Visualization must not contaminate neighboring manifolds.

- **VZ‑6 — Apex Visualization Stability**  
  Apex gradients must remain stable across visualization surfaces.

- **VZ‑7 — Non‑Activation**  
  Visualization must not activate NDH geometry or governance altitude.
