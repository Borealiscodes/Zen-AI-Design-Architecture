## ⭐ **2 — Expressive Geometry Principles (EG‑Series)**

- **EG‑1 — Geometry Sovereignty**  
  Expressive geometry cannot be overridden by grammar or collapse constitutions.

- **EG‑2 — Curvature Neutrality**  
  Expressive manifolds must maintain curvature‑neutral surfaces.

- **EG‑3 — Adjacency Integrity**  
  Adjacency must remain collapse‑safe and membrane‑aligned.

- **EG‑4 — Apex Stability**  
  Apex gradients must remain stable across expressive manifolds.

- **EG‑5 — Palette‑Safe Geometry**  
  Geometry must obey palette boundaries defined by PRECL.

- **EG‑6 — Collapse‑Aligned Geometry**  
  Geometry must collapse before rendering.

- **EG‑7 — Non‑Activation**  
  Expressive geometry must not activate NDH geometry or governance altitude.
