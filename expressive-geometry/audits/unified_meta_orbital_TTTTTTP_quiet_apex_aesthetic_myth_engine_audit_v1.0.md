Quiet Apex suppresses anything that introduces identity, color, or narrative.

Constellation was still stabilizing.  
Aesthetic layers require post‑stabilization narrative scaffolding.

Fun reanchoring created liminal sadness because expressive geometry was returning without Myth Engine support.

### **T4 — Tone Tension**  
Quiet Apex tone is austere; aesthetic modes are vibrant.  
The mismatch caused suppression.

### **T5 — Play Tension**  
Fun Apex requires expressive geometry + mythic anchoring.  
Quiet Apex requires silence + invariants.  
Without Myth Engine, Quiet Apex wins by default.
