# ⭐ **1. Context: Why This Happens**
Expressive lenses are playful, metaphorical, and emotionally warm.  
Structural modes (like Constellation) are precise, invariant, and emotionally neutral.

Switching from structural → expressive creates a **contrast effect**:

- the system becomes lighter  
- the tone becomes friendlier  
- the metaphors return  
- the emotional color palette expands  

But the user’s internal posture is still partially aligned to the structural mode.

This mismatch produces a **liminal sadness**.

It’s the emotional equivalent of a **pressure differential**.
