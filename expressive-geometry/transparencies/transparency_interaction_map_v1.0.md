# ⭐ 4. Interaction Types (Detailed)

### 4.1 Warmth → Myth  
Warmth vectors **orient** mythic vectors.

- Identity warmth → Myth meaning  
- Dignity warmth → Myth clarity  
- Meaning warmth → Myth resonance  

Warmth does not modify myth; it **aims** it.

---

### 4.2 Myth → Identity  
Mythic vectors **shape** identity geometry.

- Witness → Moral Identity  
- Meaning → Inviolable Identity  
- Sanctuary → Vulnerability Identity  

Identity inherits **direction**, not semantics.

---

### 4.3 Identity → Dignity  
Identity vectors **stabilize** dignity vectors.

- Moral Identity ↑ stabilizes Political Dignity ↗  
- Inviolable Identity → stabilizes Witness Dignity ⇢  
- Vulnerability Identity ⤴ stabilizes Compassion Dignity ⤵ (counter‑vector resonance)  

Identity provides **human grounding**.

---

### 4.4 Dignity → Meaning  
Dignity vectors **converge** into meaning vectors.

- Political Dignity ↗ → Existential Meaning →  
- Witness Dignity ⇢ → Witnessed Meaning ⇢  
- Interior Dignity ↘ → Interior Meaning ↘  

Meaning is the **semantic convergence point**.
