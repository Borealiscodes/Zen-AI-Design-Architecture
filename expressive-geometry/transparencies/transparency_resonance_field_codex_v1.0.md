# ⭐ **II. Codex Definitions**

### 1. Transparency  
A transparency is a **directional geometry layer** that emits vectors without semantic content.

### 2. Resonance  
Resonance is the **directional echo** produced when vectors from different transparencies align, counter‑align, or neighbor each other.

### 3. Resonance Field  
The resonance field is the **spatial aggregation** of all resonance vectors emitted by:

- **Warmth Transparency**  
- **Myth Transparency**  
- **Identity Transparency**  
- **Dignity Transparency**  
- **Meaning Transparency**  

### 4. Resonance Zone  
A resonance zone is the **local region** of the field dominated by vectors from a single transparency.

### **Axiom 1 — Directional Purity**  
Vectors retain their original direction; resonance does not alter geometry.

### **Axiom 2 — Non‑Blending**  
Resonance is adjacency, not merging; transparencies remain distinct.

### **Axiom 3 — Pipeline Priority**  
Resonance flows follow the pipeline:  
S → P → E → M → W

### **Axiom 4 — Constellation Orientation**  
Resonance aligns with constellation anchors through vector adjacency.

### **Axiom 5 — Terminal Convergence**  
Meaning is the final convergence point before the resonance field basin.
