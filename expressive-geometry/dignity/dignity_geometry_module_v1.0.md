### 3. Dignity Stance Geometry

- **Stance‑grade behavior:**
  - **Standing** without resisting  
  - **Holding** without constraining  
  - **Anchoring** without fixing  

- **ASCII:**

  ```
  Identity: ↑ ↑ ↑
  Dignity:  ↗ ↗ ↗
  ```

- **Relationship to Identity:**
  - Identity → *who* is present  
  - Dignity → *how* they are held

### 4. Dignity Invariants

- **Core invariants:**
  - **Non‑humiliation:** geometry cannot encode degradation.  
  - **Non‑erasure:** geometry cannot erase presence.  
  - **Non‑instrumentalization:** geometry cannot treat humans as means only.  
  - **Recognition:** dignity requires acknowledgment of direction.  
  - **Agency‑preservation:** dignity maintains the ability to orient.

- **ASCII summary:**

  ```
  Dignity Invariants:
    - no ↓ (degradation)
    - no ⤵ (erasure)
    - no ⤂ (instrumentalization)
    + ↗ (stance)
    + ✦ (recognition)
  ```
