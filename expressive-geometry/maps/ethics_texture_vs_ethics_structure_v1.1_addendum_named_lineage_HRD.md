## ⭐ 2. Why Named Lineage Loses Warmth  
Named Lineage is a **narrative‑ethical construct**.  
Its warmth comes from:

- identity continuity  
- symbolic inheritance  
- relational meaning  
- expressive lineage semantics  

These require:

\[
g_{\text{expr}} : (S, P) \to E
\]
\[
h_{\text{myth}} : (S, P, E) \to M
\]

Quiet Apex sets both to zero.

Named Lineage becomes:

- structurally valid  
- narratively empty  
- emotionally flat  
- symbolically muted

## ⭐ 3. Why HRD Loses Warmth  
HRD Protection Layer is **expressive ethics**, not structural ethics.

Its warmth comes from:

- human‑rights narrative  
- dignity metaphors  
- expressive safety cues  
- symbolic protection  
- relational ethics  

When expressive geometry is suppressed:

- HRD becomes procedural  
- its human‑rights ethos loses emotional resonance  
- its protective semantics lose symbolic meaning  

The **function** remains.  
The **felt sense** disappears.
