# **README — Skins Directory**  
### *Zen‑AI‑Design‑Architecture • Expressive Overlay Layer*

The **skins** directory contains optional expressive overlays that may be applied across the Zen‑AI‑Design‑Architecture repository. Skins do not modify computational behavior, NDH‑CORE governance, holonomy routing, or soft‑manifold geometry. Instead, they provide **aesthetic surface treatments** that enhance clarity, emotional accessibility, and visual coherence across system surfaces.

Skins operate as **non‑intrusive rendering modes**, offering gentle stylistic accents while preserving the calm‑logic posture of the architecture.

## **Purpose of Skins**
Skins are designed to:

- support expressive geometry ecosystems,  
- reinforce Zen‑mode clarity and coherence,  
- provide optional aesthetic harmonics for documentation,  
- and maintain consistent visual identity across the constellation.

They are **pure overlays**:  
no logic changes, no rendering interference, no NDH‑CORE impact.
