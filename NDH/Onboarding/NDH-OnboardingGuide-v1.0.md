### *A beginner‑friendly introduction to the NDH Dual‑Lane Framework, Serenity Basins, and SCI‑80 epistemic support*

---

## 🌲 **1. What NDH *Is***  
NDH (Non‑Dual Holonomy) is a **general epistemic framework** for exploring complex ideas using:

- **dual lanes** (rigid + soft reasoning)  
- **serenity basins** (semantic clusters)  
- **holonomy‑stable traversal** (loop‑based reasoning)  
- **forest ecology metaphors** (cognitive clarity)  
- **SCI‑80** (80‑dimensional epistemic support manifold)

It is **not a personal philosophy**.  
It is a **reusable structure** for interdisciplinary thinking, writing, and publishing.

New users can explore NDH through dual lanes, serenity basins, or SCI-80.
