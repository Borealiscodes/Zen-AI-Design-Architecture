# **2. The Three Drift Classes**
Drift in NDH‑Constellation architecture falls into three primary classes:

### **2.1 Posture Drift (Class‑P)**  
Origin: operator posture  
Signature: reactive naming, forced clarity  
Propagation: local  
Correction: awareness → naming → restoration  

### **2.2 Reflection Drift (Class‑R)**  
Origin: reflection geometry  
Signature: lengthened loops, recursion instability  
Propagation: geometric  
Correction: bounded reflection, curvature alignment  

### **2.3 Serenity Drift (Class‑S)**  
Origin: serenity basin topology  
Signature: basin flattening, basin migration  
Propagation: global  
Correction: basin deepening, posture stabilization  

These three classes form the **Drift Triad**.
