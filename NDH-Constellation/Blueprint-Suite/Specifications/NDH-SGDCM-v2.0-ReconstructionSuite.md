## **1. Trauma‑Informed Design Principles**

### **1.1 Core Principles**
- **Safety First** — No routing occurs until lane‑stability is verified.  
- **Containment Before Correction** — Debris‑fields must be stabilized before any reconstruction.  
- **Non‑Cross‑Contamination** — Forest Lane and Black Hole Lane remain isolated during recovery.  
- **Provenance Integrity** — All reconstruction actions must preserve PA‑03, PA‑07, PA‑12.  
- **Slow‑Start Dialing** — SGDCM reactivates in controlled increments.  
- **Operator‑Centered Stability** — No operator is exposed to unstable curvature or drift vectors.
