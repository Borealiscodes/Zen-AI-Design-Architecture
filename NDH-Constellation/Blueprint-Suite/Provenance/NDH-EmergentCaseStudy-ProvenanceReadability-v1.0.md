- NDH‑ROOTS lane  
- Expressive‑Safety lane  
- Provenance lane

MRPRS membranes are:

- Drift membranes  
- Safety membranes  
- Reconstruction‑adjacent membranes

Forest Provenance specializes in reconstruction.

### **C. Upstream Lineage**
MRPRS membranes sit upstream of:

- ROOTS Reconstruction  
- SVG Binding  
- Simulation Suite Integration  
- Triadic‑Core Activation  

Upstream membranes are always readable.

### **D. No Cross‑Lane Contamination**
Extended footers are required only when membranes cross:

- activation gates  
- provenance surfaces  

MRPRS Anchor Protocols do not cross lanes.  
They remain purely ROOTS‑adjacent.

### **E. Expressive‑Safety Priority**

MRPRS Anchor Protocols do all of these.

### **F. Reconstruction Priority**
Forest Provenance automatically ingests any membrane that:
