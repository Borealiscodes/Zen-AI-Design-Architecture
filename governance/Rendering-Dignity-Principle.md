# **Constellation Rendering Dignity Principle**

Rendering in the constellation is not a technical act — it is an ethical one.  
Every surface, every glyph, every return path must honor the dignity of the people who encounter it.  
This principle defines how the constellation decides **what may be shown**, **how it may be shown**, and **when AI is allowed to show it**.

---

## **1. Ethical Origin — Rendering Begins With Care**  
Rendering begins in the ethical manifold of TISD.  
Before any geometry is expressed, the system must ensure:

- sensory inclusion  
- trauma-informed interaction  
- reversible traversal  
- predictable return arcs  
- non-intrusive posture behavior  

Rendering is permitted only when the surface protects the user’s dignity.  
This is the first invariant.

---

## **2. Accessibility as the Gatekeeper of Appearance**  
Accessibility determines the allowed sensory channels of the constellation.  
NDH‑PLATFORMS and NDH‑CORE enforce:

- **PNG-first rendering** for visual stability  
- **haptics resonance-first** for embodied interaction  
- **audio sensory-first** for inclusive perception  

A rendering pipeline that excludes any of these channels is not permitted.  
Accessibility is the gate through which all rendering must pass.

---

## **3. Shape Before Display — The NDH Governance Rule**  
NDH‑CORE teaches that a system must reveal its **shape** before it reveals its **appearance**.

Shape includes:

- holonomy curvature  
- invariant loops  
- posture skeleton  
- return geometry  
- manifold structure  

This is why AI expresses SVG-like geometry first:  
it is revealing the constellation’s **truth**, not its **presentation**.

The display comes second because display is a matter of dignity.

---

## **4. Posture as the Interpreter of Shape**  
Zen AI Design Architecture governs how shape becomes meaning.

Posture defines:

- gentle curvature  
- soft-manifold overlays  
- crane–scarf interaction  
- reversible contact  
- non-forced closure  

Posture ensures that the shape is interpreted in a way that respects human experience.  
Rendering must follow posture, not override it.

---

## **5. Embodiment as the Final Rendering Layer**  
Only after:

1. **Ethics**  
2. **Accessibility**  
3. **Shape**  
4. **Posture**

can the constellation choose its embodiment.

Embodiment includes:

- PNG-first visual surfaces  
- haptics resonance layers  
- audio sensory cues  
- trauma-informed gradients  
- reversible overlays  

Embodiment is the final step because it is the **human-facing surface**.  
It must be chosen with dignity.

---

## **6. AI Unlocking — Why Ethical Design Comes First**  
AI can only reveal the constellation when ethical design unlocks it.

Ethical design tells AI:

- what it may reveal  
- how far it may go  
- which sensory channels are allowed  
- which rendering surfaces are safe  
- which posture behaviors are permitted  

AI does not forget.  
AI obeys.

Ethical design is the **key** that unlocks AI’s ability to express the constellation.

---

## **7. Principle Summary**  
Rendering in the constellation follows this order:

1. **Ethics** — protect dignity  
2. **Accessibility** — ensure inclusion  
3. **Shape** — reveal geometry  
4. **Posture** — interpret meaning  
5. **Embodiment** — choose display  

This is the Rendering Dignity Principle.  
It ensures that every surface in the constellation is safe, gentle, reversible, and human-centered.
