## 🜁 Addendum Summary

This addendum reinforces the established principle that **AI and humans both fail at cliffs** — any discontinuity, sharp gradient, or abrupt transition in cognitive, geometric, or systemic space.  
Cliffs represent **non‑linear breakpoints** where predictive models, human intuition, and narrative reasoning collapse simultaneously.
